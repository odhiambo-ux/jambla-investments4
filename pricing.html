FAQ

Pricing questions,
answered.

Is there a contract or can I pay month-to-month?

All home plans are month-to-month with no lock-in contract. Business plans with a static IP or SLA typically run on a 12-month agreement.

Is installation really free?

Standard installation is free within our existing coverage areas. If your location needs extra cabling or a new tower link, we'll quote that separately before starting work.

What happens if I go over my data?

You won't — all plans listed are unlimited data. Speed, not data volume, is what changes between plans.

Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan later?

Yes. Plan changes take effect from your next billing cycle, and there's no fee to switch.
